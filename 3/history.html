发展沿革History

从全球倡议到中国网络。From the global initiative to the China Network.

网络简介The network使命与原则Mission发展沿革History治理架构Governance领导团队Leadership
  1. 2019.12联合国贸易和发展会议与全球家族企业网络在日内瓦签署全球倡议。UNCTAD and the Family Business Network signed the global initiative in Geneva.
  2. 2021.02倡议在世界投资论坛正式启动。The initiative was launched at the World Investment Forum.
  3. 2026.09中国网络在厦门投洽会「未来投资大会」授牌成立。The China Network was inaugurated in Xiamen.
  4. 2026.10在香港发起设立运营主体。The Hong Kong operating entity was initiated.
  5. 2026.12.31创始理事确认截止。Deadline to confirm founding council members.
  6. 2027.01.22成立会议暨人工智能闭门会。Founding meeting and closed session on artificial intelligence.
  7. 2027.02首次公开活动，发布首份白皮书。此后进入常态运行：年度白皮书、年度论坛、青年项目与指标应用。First public programme and first paper. Regular work then runs through the annual paper, annual forum, youth programme and use of the indicators.
