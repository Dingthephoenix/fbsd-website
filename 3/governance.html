发起人是陈永岚、王辉耀、陈英龙。理事会现由陈永岚、陈英龙、苗绿、候朝辉组成。陈永岚任理事会主席，并任秘书长。候朝辉任执行秘书长，由理事会任命，负责日常运营。秘书长统筹对外合作与整体协调。The initiators are Chen Yonglan, Wang Huiyao and Chen Yinglong. The council is Chen Yonglan, Chen Yinglong, Miao Lu and Hou Chaohui. Chen Yonglan chairs the council and is Secretary-General. Hou Chaohui is Executive Secretary-General, appointed by the council, and runs daily operations.

专业委员会按需设立，首批方向包括企业出海法律风控和人工智能，由秘书处报理事会批准。Expert committees are added as needed. The first themes are legal and risk control for international expansion, and artificial intelligence. The secretariat proposes them and the council approves.

顾问委员会、名誉主席和荣誉理事属于荣誉体系，提供咨询与资源支持，不进入理事会，不参与决策和运营。荣誉职衔不取酬。The advisory circle, honorary chairs and honorary council titles are outside governance. They advise and open relationships. They do not sit on the council, decide, or operate the network, and the roles are unpaid.

账目将由香港执业会计师作年度审计。公司注册完成后，经审计报表按香港担保有限公司的要求提交公司注册处，并向成员提供。Accounts will be audited annually by a Hong Kong practice. After registration, audited statements will be filed as required for a company limited by guarantee and provided to members.
