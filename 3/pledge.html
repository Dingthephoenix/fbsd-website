《家族企业可持续发展承诺2030》由全球家族企业网络与联合国贸易和发展会议共同形成。签署方承诺推动可持续增长、环境守护、社会包容和良善治理，并把原则放进商业战略。The Family Business Sustainability Pledge 2030 comes from the Family Business Network and UNCTAD. Signatories commit to sustainable growth, environmental stewardship, social inclusion and good governance, and to placing those principles in business strategy.

中国网络会员均须签署这份承诺。签署与刊登在全球平台完成。本页说明中文路径，不收取签署费用，也不代替全球平台作审核。Members of the China Network sign this pledge. Signing and listing are completed on the global platform. 可持续增长Sustainable growth

推动能够带来长期价值的经营和投资。Advance business and investment that create long-term value.

环境守护Environmental stewardship

应对气候变化，采用可持续的生产和消费，审慎使用自然资源。Act on climate change, shift production and consumption, and use natural resources with care.

社会包容Social inclusion

在社区内外推动性别平等、多元和体面工作。Advance gender equality, diversity and decent work.

良善治理Good governance

建立透明、包容、可问责的家族与公司治理，遵守廉洁经营。Build transparent, inclusive and accountable family and corporate governance.
