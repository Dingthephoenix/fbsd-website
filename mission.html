把可持续发展融入商业战略，并作出可衡量的贡献。Embed sustainability in business strategy and make a measurable contribution.

网络要为传统商业模式与可持续发展提供融合路径和工具，促进可持续发展、包容性增长与长期繁荣。The network offers a path and tools that join conventional business models with sustainability, in support of sustainable development, inclusive growth and long-term prosperity.

四项原则同时构成《承诺2030》的签署内容，也是指标体系的评价维度。The four principles are both the content of the 2030 Pledge and the dimensions of the indicator set.

可持续增长Sustainable growth

以长期价值创造衡量经营成果，不以短期利润替代长期能力。Judge results by long-term value, not by short-term profit in place of lasting capability.

环境守护Environmental stewardship

把气候与资源约束纳入投资与运营决策，以技术降低环境负荷。Put climate and resource constraints into investment and operating decisions.

社会包容Social inclusion

兼顾员工、社区与供应链，让增长可共享。Include workers, communities and supply chains so growth can be shared.

良善治理Good governance

以透明、问责与制度安排保障代际传承。Secure succession through transparency, accountability and rules.
