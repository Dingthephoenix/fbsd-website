家族企业可持续发展指标（Sustainability Indicators for Family Business）建立在联合国贸发会议《核心指标指南》（2019）之上，并对齐联合国可持续发展目标。核心指标由贸发会议国际会计和报告准则政府间专家工作组认可。The Sustainability Indicators for Family Business build on UNCTAD’s 2019 Guidance on Core Indicators and are aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals. The core indicators were endorsed through UNCTAD’s intergovernmental working group on accounting and reporting.

指标在核心披露之外，补充家族企业治理、供应链、环境绩效和社区影响。企业可用它对照自身进展。全球平台上的个别填报默认不公开，汇总结果用于观察倡议整体，除非企业另行授权。Beyond the core disclosures, the set adds family-business governance, supply chains, environmental performance and community impact. Individual responses on the global platform are not published. Aggregated results describe the initiative, unless a business authorises more.

中国网络提供中文说明、行业适配讨论和报告辅导。填报系统使用全球平台，本网站不保存指标答卷。The China Network offers a Chinese explanation, discussion of sector fit, and help with reporting. The questionnaire stays on the global platform. This website does not store responses.
