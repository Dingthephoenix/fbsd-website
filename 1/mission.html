MISSION　倡议使命MISSION

未来商业领袖可持续发展协会致力于成为国际社会信赖的可持续发展协作平台，为全球企业未来发展提供传统商业模式与可持续发展相融合的路径与工具，促进可持续发展、包容性增长与长期繁荣，为联合国 2030 年可持续发展议程作出可衡量的贡献。FBSD Foundation Limited aims to become a collaboration platform for sustainable development trusted by the international community. It gives enterprises a path and tools that join conventional business models with sustainability, supporting sustainable development, inclusive growth and long-term prosperity, and a measurable contribution to the United Nations 2030 Agenda.

TOOLKIT　工具TOOLKIT

01

家族企业可持续发展承诺书 2030Family Business Sustainability Pledge 2030

FBSP2030

四大原则：可持续增长、环境守护、社会包容、良善治理。签署企业的可持续发展承诺，经倡议框架下 FBSD 官网公开刊登，面向全球展示。Four principles: sustainable growth, environmental stewardship, social inclusion and good governance. A signing enterprise’s sustainability pledge is published on the FBSD site within the initiative and shown globally.

02

家族企业可持续性指标Sustainability Indicators for Family Business

SIFB

基于 UNCTAD《核心指标指南》（2019）构建，与 SDG 对齐的家族企业衡量指标体系，为企业提供自评与对标工具，跟踪承诺落地进展。Built on UNCTAD’s Core Indicators Guidance (2019). A family-business measurement set aligned with the SDGs, for self-assessment, comparison, and tracking how the pledge is carried out.

03

FBSD 能力建设项目FBSD capacity building

CAPACITY BUILDING

面向家族企业的培训与知识共享，助力可持续理念融入经营实践；联动 FBN 全球网络与国际机构资源，构建贯通中外的学习与实践平台。Training and shared knowledge for family businesses, so sustainability enters day-to-day management. It draws on the FBN global network and international institutions, and builds a place to learn and practise across China and the world.
