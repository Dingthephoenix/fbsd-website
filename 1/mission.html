MISSION　倡议使命MISSION

为全球企业未来发展提供传统商业模式与可持续发展相融合的路径与工具，促进可持续发展、包容性增长与长期繁荣，为联合国 2030 年可持续发展议程作出可衡量的贡献。It gives enterprises a path and tools that join conventional business models with sustainability, supporting sustainable development, inclusive growth and long-term prosperity, and a measurable contribution to the United Nations 2030 Agenda.
