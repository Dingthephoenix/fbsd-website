发起人是陈永岚、王辉耀、陈英龙。其中陈永岚任筹委会主任，王辉耀、陈英龙任筹委会委员。理事会现由陈永岚、陈英龙、苗绿、候朝辉组成，四位理事同时是公司的初始成员。陈永岚任理事会主席，并任秘书长，统筹对外合作与整体协调。候朝辉任执行秘书长，负责日常运营。The initiators are Chen Yonglan, Wang Huiyao and Chen Yinglong. Chen Yonglan chairs the preparatory committee. Wang Huiyao and Chen Yinglong are members of it. The council is Chen Yonglan, Chen Yinglong, Miao Lu and Hou Chaohui. The four council members are also the company’s initial members. Chen Yonglan chairs the council and is Secretary-General, coordinating external cooperation. Hou Chaohui is Executive Secretary-General and runs daily operations.

专业委员会按需设立，方向包括企业出海法律风控和人工智能，由秘书处报理事会批准后设立。Professional committees are added as needed, including legal and risk control for going global, and artificial intelligence. The secretariat proposes them and the council approves.

荣誉体系与治理职务分开，不进入理事会，不参与决策和运营，也不取酬。领袖席位采用邀请制，包括荣誉主席、联席主席、资深副主席和副主席，只受邀授予、不设付费；可以列席理事会会议、听取执行秘书长的工作汇报。会员席位面向会员，按贡献授予常务理事和理事，提供咨询与资源支持。Honorary roles stay outside governance. They do not sit on the council, decide, operate the network, or receive pay. Leader seats are by invitation only: honorary chair, co-chair, senior vice-chair and vice-chair. They are not for sale. Holders may attend council meetings and hear the Executive Secretary-General’s report. Member seats, standing council member and council member, are granted to members according to contribution, and offer advice and relationships.

账目由香港执业会计师作年度审计。收入专款专用。公司注册完成后，经审计报表提交公司注册处，并向成员提供。Accounts are audited annually by a Hong Kong practice. Funds are used for the purpose they were given. After registration, audited statements are filed with the Companies Registry and provided to members.
