2019.12

日内瓦Geneva

FBSD 全球倡议框架文件于日内瓦签署，确立贸发会议（UNCTAD）与家族企业网络（FBN）联合推动的合作框架。The FBSD global initiative framework was signed in Geneva, setting the cooperation framework jointly taken forward by UNCTAD and the Family Business Network (FBN).

2021.02

于 UNCTAD 世界投资论坛正式启动，倡议进入全球推广阶段。It was launched at the UNCTAD World Investment Forum, and the initiative entered its global promotion.

2026.10

多哈Doha
