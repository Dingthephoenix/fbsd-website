发展沿革History

从全球倡议到中国网络。From the global initiative to the China Network.

网络简介The network使命与原则Mission发展沿革History治理架构Governance领导团队Leadership
  1. 2019.12.06联合国贸易和发展会议与全球家族企业网络在日内瓦签署框架文件。UNCTAD and the Family Business Network signed the framework in Geneva.
  2. 2021.02.25倡议在联合国贸发会议世界投资论坛上正式启动。The initiative was launched at the UNCTAD World Investment Forum.
  3. 2026.09中国网络在厦门投洽会「2026 未来投资大会」授牌成立。陈永岚任筹委会主任，王辉耀、陈英龙任委员。The China Network was inaugurated in Xiamen. Chen Yonglan chairs the preparatory committee. Wang Huiyao and Chen Yinglong are members.
  4. 2026.10在香港发起设立非营利担保有限公司，作为中国网络的运营主体。A Hong Kong non-profit company limited by guarantee was initiated as the operating entity.
  5. 2026三项工作并行：完成公司注册并组建秘书处基础团队，同步提交第 88 条申请；启动创始会员与专家顾问招募；启动首次活动策划和白皮书预研。Three tracks run together: register the company and form a core secretariat, and file the section 88 application; recruit founding members and expert advisers; begin planning the first programme and the white paper.
  6. 2027.01.22成立会议暨人工智能闭门会。Founding meeting and closed session on artificial intelligence.
  7. 2027 一季度首批创始会员与专家顾问到位，并组建顾问委员会。2027 年 2 月前举办首次活动并发布首份白皮书。此后进入常态运行：年度白皮书、研讨交流、青年培养与国际协同。The first founding members and expert advisers are in place, and the advisory committee is formed. The first programme and the first paper are planned before February 2027. Regular work then runs through the annual paper, dialogue, youth development and international coordination.
