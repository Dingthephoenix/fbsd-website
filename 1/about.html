联合国贸易和发展组织家族企业促进可持续发展中国网络，英文 Family Business for Sustainable Development China Network。2026 年 9 月于厦门投洽会「2026 未来投资大会」授牌成立。筹委会主任为陈永岚，委员为王辉耀、陈英龙。The Family Business for Sustainable Development China Network was inaugurated in September 2026 at the 2026 Future Investment Conference during the China International Fair for Investment and Trade in Xiamen. Chen Yonglan chairs the preparatory committee. Wang Huiyao and Chen Yinglong are members.

网络以未来企业为核心牵引，汇聚企业与各界力量，覆盖香港、澳门、台湾和中国内地。服务对象包括家族企业、家族办公室、上市公司、学术机构和专业服务机构。工作沿白皮书、研讨交流、青年培养、国际协同四个板块推进，并与联合国贸发会议及全球网络保持联动。Future-facing enterprises are the centre of the work. The network brings together business and other partners across Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and the Chinese mainland. It serves family businesses, family offices, listed companies, academic bodies and professional firms. The work runs in four streams: white papers, dialogue, youth development and international coordination, in step with UNCTAD and the global network.

法律与运营主体是未来企业促进可持续发展有限公司，英文 Future Business for Sustainable Development (FBSD) Limited，在香港设立的非营利担保有限公司。它是中国网络的法律承载，也是日后与联合国贸发会议签署合作协议的主体。成员责任以担保额为限，利润不分派，资源用于宗旨范围内的工作。2026 年内完成注册并组建秘书处基础团队，同步申请香港《税务条例》第 88 条慈善团体免税资格。该资格须在注册后另行申请，本站不宣称已经取得。The legal and operating entity is Future Business for Sustainable Development (FBSD) Limited, a Hong Kong non-profit company limited by guarantee. It carries the China Network in law, and is the entity that will sign a cooperation agreement with UNCTAD. Liability is limited to the guarantee, profits are not distributed, and resources stay within the purpose. Registration and a core secretariat team are planned within 2026, together with an application for charitable exemption under section 88 of the Inland Revenue Ordinance. That status is applied for after registration. This site does not claim it has been granted.

公开活动不直接以家族企业或家族财富为题，统一用中性公共议题命名，家族企业身份不进入活动名称和主视觉。政策对齐的议题包括减碳、气候变化、生态保护和企业出海，并对接国家「十五五」规划与香港发展议程。前沿科技议题包括人工智能、脑机接口和具身智能。气候与生态对应「环境守护」，出海与增长对应「可持续增长」和包容性增长。Public programmes are not titled around family business or family wealth. They use neutral public subjects, and family-business identity stays out of the name and the main visual. Policy topics are decarbonisation, climate, ecological protection and going global, aligned with the 15th Five-Year Plan and Hong Kong’s development agenda. Frontier topics include artificial intelligence, brain–computer interfaces and embodied intelligence. Climate and ecology sit with environmental stewardship. Going global and growth sit with sustainable and inclusive growth.
