FBSD Foundation Limited（未来商业领袖可持续发展协会）是一家在香港注册成立的非营利担保有限公司，立足香港这一连接中国与全球市场的国际枢纽，推动产业领军机构、长期资本机构与专业服务机构参与可持续发展行动。FBSD Foundation Limited (未来商业领袖可持续发展协会) is a non-profit company limited by guarantee, registered in Hong Kong. From Hong Kong, a hub between China and global markets, it brings industrial leaders, long-term capital and professional firms into sustainability work.

协会承接联合国贸易和发展会议（UNCTAD）与家族企业网络（FBN）于 2021 年联合发起的 Family Business for Sustainable Development（家族企业促进可持续发展，FBSD）全球倡议，作为 FBSD 中国网络的承接主体，推动企业将可持续发展纳入核心战略。The association carries forward the Family Business for Sustainable Development (FBSD) initiative launched in 2021 by UNCTAD and the Family Business Network (FBN). It is the body that carries the FBSD China Network, and it works to bring sustainability into core business strategy.

协会以未来商业领袖为核心牵引，汇聚企业与各界力量，在大中华区推动倡议落地，连接国际平台与本土实践，助力中国贡献全球可持续发展议程。Future business leaders are at the centre. The association brings together enterprises and other partners, takes the initiative across Greater China, and joins the international platform with practice at home, so that China’s contribution can enter the global sustainability agenda.

机构性质Form

依香港《公司条例》注册的非营利担保有限公司，利润不作分配，保障公益属性。A non-profit company limited by guarantee, registered under the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance. Profits are not distributed.
