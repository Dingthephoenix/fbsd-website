名称Names

对外名称是「联合国贸易和发展组织家族企业促进可持续发展中国网络」。这表示网络承接联合国贸易和发展会议与全球家族企业网络的倡议。它不是联合国的机构、基金或办事处，不能代表联合国表态，也不能把联合国或贸发会议的标志用在会员自己的宣传上。The public name is the UNCTAD Family Business for Sustainable Development China Network. It means the network carries forward an initiative of UNCTAD and the Family Business Network. It is not an office, fund or organ of the United Nations. It cannot speak for the United Nations, and members may not place the United Nations or UNCTAD emblem on their own materials.

法律主体的中文名称是未来企业促进可持续发展有限公司，英文 Future Business for Sustainable Development (FBSD) Limited。公司在香港设立，目前办理注册。第 88 条免税资格需在注册后另行申请，本站不宣称已经取得。The legal entity is Future Business for Sustainable Development (FBSD) Limited. It is being registered in Hong Kong. Section 88 charitable status has to be applied for after registration. This site does not claim that status.
