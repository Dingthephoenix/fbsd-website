网络简介The network

对外以中国网络名义开展合作与活动，覆盖大中华区。The public name is the China Network. It works across Greater China.

联合国贸易和发展组织家族企业促进可持续发展中国网络，英文 Family Business for Sustainable Development China Network。2026 年 9 月于厦门投洽会「未来投资大会」授牌成立。The Family Business for Sustainable Development China Network was inaugurated in September 2026 at the Future Investment Conference during the China International Fair for Investment and Trade in Xiamen.

网络覆盖香港、澳门、台湾和中国内地，服务家族企业、家族办公室、上市公司和专业服务机构。工作方式是标准、研究、培训、对话四条主线并行。It covers Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and the Chinese mainland, and serves family businesses, family offices, listed companies and professional firms. The work runs on four lines: standards, research, training and dialogue.

法律与运营主体是未来企业促进可持续发展有限公司，英文 Future Business for Sustainable Development (FBSD) Limited，在香港设立的非营利担保有限公司。成员责任以担保额为限，利润不分派。公司注册完成后，将同步申请香港《税务条例》第 88 条慈善团体免税资格。The legal and operating entity is Future Business for Sustainable Development (FBSD) Limited, a Hong Kong non-profit company limited by guarantee. Liability is limited to the guarantee, and profits are not distributed. After registration, the company will apply for charitable exemption under section 88 of the Inland Revenue Ordinance.

公开活动以企业出海、气候变化、减碳和科技等议题命名。家族企业是服务对象，不作为活动名称。Public programmes are named for topics such as international expansion, climate, decarbonisation and technology. Family business is who the network serves, not the title of an event.
